Telegram not working? Here's how to unblock it

If Telegram won't connect, is stuck loading, or was blocked in your country, a free MTProto proxy fixes it in a few minutes. No VPN, no subscription, no account.

Why has Telegram stopped working?

When Telegram suddenly won't connect — messages won't send, media won't load, or it just spins forever — it's usually not your phone. Your internet provider or country is blocking or slowing Telegram. This happens during protests, elections, exams, or ongoing censorship.

The fix is a Telegram proxy: a server in another country that passes your Telegram traffic along, so the block no longer sees it. Telegram has its own kind, called MTProto, and people share these proxies publicly for free.

The catch: those public lists go stale within hours, so most proxies you find are already dead. Nimku Proxy solves that — it tests every proxy and shows only the ones working right now.

Step 1 — Install the app

  1. Download NimkuProxy.apk from the release page.
  2. Open the downloaded file. Android will ask permission to install — tap Settings → Allow from this source → Install.
  3. Xiaomi, Huawei and Samsung phones need a couple of extra toggles — see the install guide.

Step 2 — Get working proxies

There are two ways, and the second one works even when everything else is blocked:

A. Let the app find them

Open the app, pick a scan depth, and press start. It gathers proxies from dozens of public sources and tests each one live. Best for most people.

B. Get a file from the Telegram bot (if GitHub is blocked)

In some regions the app can't reach the sources because GitHub itself is blocked. Telegram usually still works — so open @Nimkubot, press Get proxy list, and it sends you a file. Tap it → Open with → Nimku Proxy, and the app checks that list instead.

Step 3 — Connect

  1. The app shows working proxies, fastest at the top.
  2. Tap one. Telegram opens and asks to enable the proxy.
  3. Confirm — and Telegram works again.

Every proxy is tested on your network, so a proxy that shows as working really works for you. If one dies later, just open the app and pick another.

Is it safe?

Nimku Proxy is open source — anyone can read the code — and has no ads, no tracking, and no Google or Firebase services. It doesn't touch your Telegram account or your messages; it only hands a proxy to your Telegram app. Every release can be checked on VirusTotal and verified by its signing certificate. Details are on the main page.

One honest note: public proxies are run by strangers, so don't treat a proxy as a privacy tool — your Telegram chats stay encrypted between you and Telegram, but use it to reach Telegram, not to hide who you are.

Frequently asked questions

Is a proxy the same as a VPN?

No. A VPN routes everything on your phone; an MTProto proxy is used only by Telegram, so your other apps are untouched. It's lighter and made for Telegram specifically.

Do I need to pay?

No. The app and the proxies are completely free — no subscription, no account, no ads.

The proxy stopped working — what now?

Normal. Open the app, scan again or get a fresh file from @Nimkubot, and pick another. Lists refresh every hour.

Does it work on iPhone?

The app is Android only. On iPhone you can still use @Nimkubot to get proxy links and add them to Telegram manually.
