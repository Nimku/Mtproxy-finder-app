Why you need it

In many countries Telegram is blocked, throttled, or simply refuses to connect. A Telegram MTProto proxy fixes that — it is a server somewhere else in the world that passes your Telegram traffic along for you.

People publish these proxies for free in lists and channels. The problem is that those lists die fast. A proxy that worked this morning is gone by evening: overloaded, blocked, or switched off. Most addresses in any public list are already dead when you find them, and testing them one by one by hand is slow and frustrating.

Nimku Proxy does that work for you.

How it works

  1. Collects proxy addresses from dozens of public lists and Telegram channels.
  2. Tests each one for real — a genuine MTProto handshake, run from your phone on your network. A list's own "verified" label is never trusted.
  3. Shows only working proxies, fastest at the top, appearing live while the scan is still running.
  4. You tap one and Telegram opens and asks you to switch it on.

That second step is the one that matters. A proxy is only "working" from your network — one that is fine in one country may be blocked in another. Because the test runs on your device, the results are true for you specifically.

What it is not

It is not a VPN. It does not route your phone's traffic anywhere. It finds proxy addresses and hands the one you pick to Telegram. Only Telegram uses it — your browser and other apps are unaffected.

It does not touch your Telegram account. No login, no phone number, no access to your messages.

It collects nothing about you. No account, no analytics, no trackers, no ads, no Google or Firebase services.

Works even where GitHub is blocked

The app normally fetches its proxy lists from GitHub. In some regions GitHub itself is unreachable — and that varies by city and mobile operator. For those cases there is a second path that needs no GitHub access at all: our Telegram bot sends you the current proxy list as a plain file, you tap it, choose Open with → Nimku Proxy, and the app checks that list exactly like a normal scan.

Is it safe to install?

You should not install an unknown APK on trust, and you do not have to. Every release can be verified in several ways:

  • Scan the APK yourself on VirusTotal or with any antivirus you trust
  • Compare the published SHA-256 checksum with your download
  • Check the signing certificate — every release is signed with the same key
  • Build it yourself from source and compare the compiled code

Frequently asked questions

What is a Telegram MTProto proxy?

MTProto is Telegram's own proxy protocol. An MTProto proxy is a server that passes your Telegram traffic along for you, letting Telegram connect where it is blocked or throttled. Only Telegram uses it — it is not a VPN.

Is it really free?

Yes, completely. No subscription, no payment, no account, no sign-up, no advertising.

Why don't proxies from public lists work?

Public lists go stale within hours — proxies get overloaded, blocked, or switched off. Most addresses in a published list are already dead. Nimku Proxy tests every address itself, right now, from your network.

Does it collect my data?

No. No account, no login, no analytics, no trackers, no Google or Firebase services. Your scan results and favourites stay on your phone, and the source code is public so you can check.

Are public proxies safe to use?

Be aware that public proxies are run by strangers. Whoever operates one can see that you are connecting and how much traffic you send. Your Telegram chats stay encrypted between you and Telegram, and secret chats stay end-to-end encrypted, but a proxy is not an anonymity tool. Use it to reach Telegram when it is blocked, not to hide who you are.
